Tempo, clock and metronome

The global clock sets the time for everything that plays in rhythm: the sequencer, the arpeggiator, the loop banks, the delay in beat sync and the metronome. Its tempo, transport, time signature, metronome and count-in are all in the top bar.

BPM

Section titled “BPM”

Tap the BPM chip in the top bar to change the tempo: − and + step by one, holding either repeats, and you can type a value. The same value is in Settings → Audio → Playback & MIDI → BPM (TEMPO).

Range Default
40 to 240 BPM 120 BPM

The tempo drives the drum sequencer, both arpeggiators, the delay in beat sync, the metronome, loop quantizing and audio loop playback speed.

Transport

Section titled “Transport”
Control Key What it does
Clock counter The clock’s position, as bars.beats.sixteenths, starting at 0.0.00
▶ Play / Pause Enter, or Space with no loop bank selected Starts the clock, or pauses it where it is
■ Stop Esc First press: pauses the clock, loop banks and drum sequencer. Second press: resets the counter to 0.0.00

Recording a blank loop bank or starting the session recorder also starts the clock.

Time signature

Section titled “Time signature”

The chip next to BPM, such as 4/4.

Part Options Default
Beats per bar 1 to 16 4
Beat unit 2, 4, 8, 16 4

A beat lasts 60 ÷ BPM seconds whatever the beat unit, so 120 BPM is 500 ms per beat in 4/4 and in 6/8 alike. The number of beats per bar changes how long a bar is, which changes everything measured in bars: quantize, count-in, the arpeggiator’s speeds and the metronome.

Metronome

Section titled “Metronome”

The metronome chip in the transport has a switch and an arrow.

  • The switch turns the click on and off.
  • The arrow opens the GLOBAL settings:
Setting Options What it does
COUNT-IN The same options as Count In below Clicks before the clock starts or a blank bank records
METRONOME NONE, 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1, 2 (bars) How often it clicks
VOLUME 0 to 100% Click level

Count In

Section titled “Count In”

Count In: Plays metronome clicks for this long before the global clock starts or a blank bank records. Needs the metronome on, and is skipped if a loop is already playing.

From the in-app help.

The count-in length is shared: it’s the same setting in the Loop Banks header (COUNT IN) and in the metronome’s COUNT-IN.
