MIDI effects

Play: knows synths and loopsDeep: exact behavior

The MIDI EFFECTS panel changes the notes you play before they reach the synth or the MIDI port. It holds one effect, Diatonic Chords.

Diatonic Chords

Section titled “Diatonic Chords”

When it’s on, every note you play on the XY pad also plays extra notes above or below it. The extra notes are counted in steps of the current scale, so the chord always fits the key. “Diatonic” means “from the scale”.

Control What it does
DIATONIC CHORDS switch Turns chords on or off. The first time, it starts with Parallel Thirds
Preset Picks a chord shape, or Custom
Offsets (Diatonic steps) One slider per added note: drag it to set how many scale steps the note sits above (+) or below (−) the note you play
+ Add Note Adds another note to the chord
× Removes that note
Scale Notes Reference Opens a table of the scale’s notes with their degree and MIDI number

Chord notes light up purple on the XY pad.

Chord shapes

Section titled “Chord shapes”

Offsets are in scale steps: +2 means two notes up the scale. In a 7-note scale, +2 and +4 from the root make a triad; in the Chromatic scale the shapes use semitones instead, so they come out as the named chord.

ChordAdded notes, in scale stepsIn Chromatic, in semitones
Parallel Thirds (-2 / +2)-2, +2-2, +2
Major Triad (1-3-5 / 1-b3-5)+2, +4+4, +7
Seventh (1-3-5-7)+2, +4, +6+4, +7, +11
Ninth (1-3-5-7-9)+2, +4, +6, +8+4, +7, +11, +14
Suspended 4th (1-4-5)+3, +4+5, +7
Octave (+1 Oct)+7+12
Octave Duo (-1/+1 Oct)-7, +7-12, +12
Power Chord (1-5)+4+7

In scales with fewer or more than seven notes, Octave and Octave Duo still move by a whole octave: the offset becomes the number of notes in that scale.

How chords interact with the rest of the app

Section titled “How chords interact with the rest of the app”
  • HOLD latches the whole chord.
  • The synth arpeggiator plays chord notes as part of its cycle.
  • In MIDI output mode, the chord notes are sent as MIDI notes.
  • Each chord note counts toward MAX VOICES.
