Mixer

Play: knows synths and loopsDeep: exact behavior

The mixer is the row of knobs at the top left, with a meter row for each channel beside them.

KnobMeter rowControlsStarts at
MASTERMSTEverything you hear: instruments, mic and loop banks.70%
SYNTHSYNThe XY pad synth, wet and dry, before the input mixer.70%
PROGPRGEffects return: everything that went through Audio Effects, before it joins the dry signals.70%
DRUMSDRMThe drum pads and sequencer, wet and dry, before the input mixer.70%
LOOPSLOOPPlayback of every loop bank.70%
INPUTINThe microphone or line input, when it is turned on.50%

For SYNTH and DRUMS, 70% is unity, the instrument’s natural level, and the range above it is headroom.

Using a knob

Section titled “Using a knob”

Press on a knob and drag up to raise it or down to lower it. A drag of about 100 pixels covers the whole range.

MASTER

Drag up or down on the knob. Value: 70%

Meters

Section titled “Meters”
Meter row Measures
MST After the MASTER knob
SYN After the SYNTH knob
PRG After the PROG knob
DRM After the DRUMS knob
LOOP After the LOOPS knob
IN After the INPUT knob

Each row uses its channel’s colour at normal levels, orange as the level gets hot, and red near clipping.

Where the channels sit

Section titled “Where the channels sit”

SYNTH and DRUMS set each instrument’s level before the Input Mixer splits it into wet (through the effects) and dry. PROG sets the level of everything coming out of the Audio Effects chain. The loop banks record after these, so a loop has the levels you were using when you recorded it, and LOOPS only sets how loud loops play back.

The full routing is on How sound flows through the app.
