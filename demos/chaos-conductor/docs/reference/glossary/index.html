Glossary

Start: no music background

Each term has a plain meaning, then what it means in this app.

Arpeggiator: Plays held notes one at a time in a repeating pattern. In the app: the Arpeggiator panel, with separate Synth and Drums arpeggiators.

Audition: Hear a sound on its own to check it. In the app: the play button in the pad editor, and the sequencer’s AUDITION switch.

Bar: A group of beats; in 4/4 a bar is four beats. In the app: the first number in the clock counter, and the unit for Quantize, Count In and arpeggiator speeds.

BPM: Beats per minute, the tempo. In the app: the BPM chip, 40 to 240.

Bus: A path where several sounds are mixed together. In the app: the instrument bus mixes the synth, drums and input before the master level.

Choke group: A set of drum pads that cut each other off, like open and closed hi-hats. In the app: the pad editor’s Choke setting.

Clipping: Distortion when a signal is too loud for the output. In the app: the red end of a meter.

Count-in: Clicks before recording starts, so you know when to come in. In the app: COUNT IN in the Loop Banks header.

Diatonic: Using only the notes of the current scale. In the app: Diatonic Chords builds chords from scale steps.

Dry / wet: The sound without effects (dry) and with them (wet). In the app: the Input Mixer card, and the Mix parameter on several effects.

Gate: How long a note sounds within its step. In the app: the arpeggiator’s Time mode.

Latency: The delay between an action and hearing it. In the app: shown as “out” in AUDIO STATUS.

Loop bank: A slot that records a loop and repeats it. In the app: the four banks in the Loop Banks panel.

MIDI: A standard way for music devices and apps to send notes and controls to each other. It carries no sound itself. In the app: OUTPUT MODE → MIDI Output.

MIDI CC (control change): A MIDI message that moves a knob or setting on the receiving instrument. In the app: the Y axis sends CC in MIDI mode.

Octave: The distance from one note to the next note of the same name, such as C3 to C4. In the app: OCTAVES RANGE, 1 to 8.

Overdub: Record on top of an existing loop, keeping what’s there. In the app: tap a playing bank, or hold one with RECORD armed.

Polyphony, voices: How many notes can sound at once. In the app: MAX VOICES, 1 to 10.

Portamento, pitch slide: Gliding from one note’s pitch to the next instead of jumping. In the app: PITCH SLIDE, in solo mode.

Quantize: Snap timing to a grid so events land in time. In the app: QUANTIZE delays bank taps to the next grid point.

Root key: The note a scale starts on, its home note. In the app: ROOT KEY, on the SCALE chip.

Sample: A short recording played back as an instrument sound. In the app: each drum pad plays a sample; load your own with LOAD.

Scale: A set of notes that sound good together. In the app: SELECTED SCALE decides which notes are on the XY pad.

Semitone: The smallest step between notes on a piano, such as C to C#. In the app: the unit of a drum pad’s PITCH.

Sequencer: A grid that plays a programmed pattern. In the app: the 16-step drum sequencer.

Signal chain: The order a sound passes through effects. In the app: the Audio Effects cards, top to bottom.

Sixteenth note: A quarter of a beat in 4/4. In the app: the last number in the clock counter, and one sequencer step.

Subdivision: A division of the beat or bar. In the app: the metronome’s METRONOME rate.

Time signature: Beats per bar over the note value of one beat, such as 4/4 or 6/8. In the app: the chip next to BPM.

VU meter: A bar showing how loud a signal is. In the app: the meter rows next to the mixer knobs.

Waveform: The shape of the synth’s raw sound wave, which sets its basic tone. In the app: WAVEFORM in the settings.
