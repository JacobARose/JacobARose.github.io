Changelog

Each version lists New features, Changes to how existing features behave, and Bug fixes separately. Internal work, such as refactoring and tests, is left out. Versions with only internal work are listed at the end.

v2.77

Section titled “v2.77”

New features

Section titled “New features”
  • Dual arpeggiator. The Arpeggiator panel has Synth and Drums tabs, each with its own on/off, Time/Speed mode and value.
  • Note number labels. Note labels can show each note’s place in the scale (I, II, III…).
  • Settings groups. The settings panel is split into Audio and Appearance, and has an Audio status line.
  • Note color picker. A built-in hue and lightness picker replaces the browser’s slower one.

Changes

Section titled “Changes”
  • Note labels default to Note name.
  • Deleting a loop needs a 0.5 s hold, down from 2 s.
  • The XY pad’s HOLD button is only in the top bar.

Bug fixes

Section titled “Bug fixes”
  • The Drum Kits header buttons (Presets, EDIT, SEQ/PAD, maximize) did nothing, and SEQ/PAD ran off the side panel.
  • The routing popover was cut off on screens narrower than 1980 px.
  • Panels couldn’t scroll with two or more notes held on the XY pad, and dragging a knob or slider could scroll its panel.
  • A click on note release when the filter returned to rest.
  • After another app used the audio, sound could stop while the app thought it was still running. The next tap now restarts it.
  • Effect card titles overlapped in side panels, collapsed horizontal cards lost their arrow, and expanded horizontal cards didn’t scroll.

v2.75

Section titled “v2.75”

New features

Section titled “New features”
  • Hold to overdub. With RECORD armed, holding a playing bank records onto it only while held, exactly where you played.
  • Hold to erase. With ERASE armed, holding a playing bank silences the part of the loop that passes while held, for audio and MIDI loops.
  • Hold to delete. Deleting a loop needs DELETE armed and a hold, with a progress ring.
  • Loop Length. Preselect a loop length from 1/4 note to 16 whole notes, or FREE. A preset length records exactly that long, then goes into overdub.

Changes

Section titled “Changes”
  • The mode buttons sit on a rail beside the banks, with Loop Length, Quantize and Count In together in the Loop Banks header.
  • Holding a bank with no mode armed no longer does anything.
  • Clearer session record button: neutral when idle, solid with a REC label when recording, pause bars and PAUSED when paused.
  • Help text for every new and changed control.

Bug fixes

Section titled “Bug fixes”
  • Overdub audio was always mixed in from the start of the loop instead of where it was played.
  • Banks recording at the same time shared one recorder.
  • The Signal Flow diagram couldn’t be scrolled by touch.
  • Audio Effects controls didn’t respond while a finger was on the XY pad.
  • A black gap between Loop Banks and MIDI Effects on devices with a home indicator.

v2.74

Section titled “v2.74”

New features

Section titled “New features”
  • DRUMS mixer channel. A knob and meter row for the drums. The default level matches the earlier loudness, with headroom above.

Changes

Section titled “Changes”
  • The panel layout choice is remembered between visits.
  • HOLD can be switched on while already touching the XY pad.
  • Drum pads can be auditioned from inside the pad editor.
  • Mixer knob and meter colours match each other, and the meters are in the same order as the knobs.

Bug fixes

Section titled “Bug fixes”
  • The arpeggiator drifted against loops over time, and ran at the wrong speed in time signatures other than 4/4.
  • The metronome menu was cut off on screens narrower than 1980 px.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the right edge of the top bar and panel arrows were cut off, and the installed app was letterboxed in landscape.
  • The key/scale popover was too narrow, the arpeggiator title was clipped, and effect card titles overlapped their arrows in the bottom layout.

v2.73b

Section titled “v2.73b”

New features

Section titled “New features”
  • Offline support. The app can be installed and works offline, including the factory drum samples, and updates itself when a new version is available.

v2.73a

Section titled “v2.73a”

New features

Section titled “New features”
  • LOOP meter. A meter row for loop playback.
  • Collapsible Input Mixer. The Input Mixer card can be collapsed, stays at the top of the chain, and remembers its state.
  • Sequencer window highlight. Steps outside the Divide/Move window are dimmed.
  • AUDITION switch for hearing pads as you add sequencer steps.

Changes

Section titled “Changes”
  • All on/off switches share one clearer design, with a visible off state.
  • In solo mode with HOLD on, lifted fingers stay part of the held note.
  • Drum pads show their sample’s name.
  • The onboarding tour uses spotlight rings instead of gesture animations.

Bug fixes

Section titled “Bug fixes”
  • The Closed Hat sample was missing.
  • Side panel titles were clipped, and top bar buttons ran off the screen.

v2.73

Section titled “v2.73”

New features

Section titled “New features”
  • Sequencer in the background. The drum sequencer keeps playing when you switch back to the pads, and has its own mute switch.
  • Divide and Move. Two knobs loop a window inside the 16-step pattern.
  • Wet/dry Input Mixer. The synth and the drums each have a wet amount: how much goes through the effects.
  • LOOPS knob. Loop playback has its own level.

Bug fixes

Section titled “Bug fixes”
  • Multi-touch on the XY pad could stop updating.

v2.72a

Section titled “v2.72a”

Changes

Section titled “Changes”
  • When there’s no room for every open panel, the least recently used one closes.

Bug fixes

Section titled “Bug fixes”
  • Pitch slide in solo mode didn’t glide.
  • Effect mappings sometimes didn’t update on the pad.
  • Drum sequencer: accidental drags while tapping steps.

v2.72

Section titled “v2.72”

New features

Section titled “New features”
  • Rotary knobs for the mixer and drum controls.
  • Floating pad editor that can be dragged aside, with a knob for each setting.
  • Two-finger pitch slide in solo mode.
  • Kit bar: LOAD, previous and next kit, and shuffle.
  • Drag to draw sequencer steps.

Changes

Section titled “Changes”
  • New default Y-axis mappings for each effect.

Bug fixes

Section titled “Bug fixes”
  • Arrows in the signal-flow diagram met in the wrong places.

v2.71b

Section titled “v2.71b”

New features

Section titled “New features”
  • Drum kit presets: save, load and manage kits.
  • Pad editor for each drum pad.
  • Drums arpeggiator: held drum pads can be arpeggiated.
  • Signal Flow diagram of the audio routing.
  • Pitch slide in solo mode, and a metronome volume.
  • Collapsible Arpeggiator panel, a maximize mode for Drum Kits, and a menu button in the top bar.

v2.71a

Section titled “v2.71a”

Bug fixes

Section titled “Bug fixes”
  • The Drum Kits header had a dead zone, the preset list conflicted with other controls, a side panel arrow was missing, and the settings button could be off screen in landscape.
  • On iPad: the metronome popup, chip popovers and drum list scrolling misbehaved.
  • The count-in played with the metronome off.

v2.7

Section titled “v2.7”

New features

Section titled “New features”
  • Drum sampler. Sixteen sample pads with per-pad level, pan, pitch, decay and choke groups, and a glue compressor.
  • Drum pads and sequencer. A 4×4 pad grid where height on the pad sets velocity, keyboard keys for every pad, and a 16-step sequencer.
  • VHS Beats Kit A and Kit B.
  • Metronome chip with a beat pulse, count-in and click rate.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Space for play/stop, R for record, and a key for each drum pad.
  • Onboarding tour with keyboard navigation.

Bug fixes

Section titled “Bug fixes”
  • The Routing, Scale and BPM chips responded poorly to touch.
  • The top bar overflowed on narrow portrait screens and iPad.
  • Help mode appeared under other elements and didn’t turn off properly.

  • Collapsible top bar.
  • Horizontal meters, taking much less space.
  • Audio blocked banner when the browser stops the app from making sound.

Changes

Section titled “Changes”
  • Side panels share the available height in landscape.

v2.42

Section titled “v2.42”

Changes

Section titled “Changes”
  • Tap a bank’s outer edge to select it.
  • The first Stop pauses; the second stops loops and resets the clock.
  • Long scale names wrap instead of being cut off.
  • Clearer paused and selected banks, and larger loop counters.

Bug fixes

Section titled “Bug fixes”
  • The first touch could be silent while audio started.
  • Loop banks could get stuck between states, and reverse playback wasn’t quantized.
  • Sliders jumped when you started dragging.

v2.41

Section titled “v2.41”

New features

Section titled “New features”

  • Loop counters under each bank, in bars or seconds.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for loop banks and the transport.

Bug fixes

Section titled “Bug fixes”
  • A bank’s Stop button could leave it playing.
  • Loops grew longer after overdubbing.
  • Double-tap zoom on iOS.
  • Phantom notes after the page came back from the background.
