Playing

Play: knows synths and loops

Play smooth mono leads with pitch slide

Section titled “Play smooth mono leads with pitch slide”

A mono (one-note) synth that glides from note to note, like a classic lead line.

  1. Open ≡ Settings → Audio → Synthesizer.

  2. Set MAX VOICES to 1 (SOLO).

  3. Drag PITCH SLIDE to the right. The label shows the glide time, from 0 ms (an instant jump) up to 500 ms.

  4. Play the pad and jump between notes. Each new note slides up or down from the last one.

Two-finger slide: in solo mode with the arpeggiator off, put a second finger down while the first is still held. The note glides to a point between your two fingers, so moving either one bends the pitch. The pad draws one cursor that follows the sliding note.

Note

PITCH SLIDE only applies when MAX VOICES is 1. With more voices, the label reads “mono only” and the slider has no effect.

Play chords with one finger

Section titled “Play chords with one finger”

  1. On the left edge, open MIDI EFFECTS.

  2. Turn on DIATONIC CHORDS. The first time, it starts with Parallel Thirds.

  3. Choose a chord from the Preset list, such as Major Triad. Every note you play now brings its chord with it, built from notes in the current scale. Chord notes light up purple on the pad.

  4. For your own shape, choose Custom and tap + Add Note. Drag each note’s slider to set how many scale steps it sits above or below the note you play, and tap × to remove one.

Because the chord is built from the scale, a “Major Triad” in a minor key comes out minor where the scale says so. The full list is in MIDI Effects.

Play more or fewer notes at once

Section titled “Play more or fewer notes at once”

MAX VOICES (Settings → Audio → Synthesizer) sets how many notes sound together, from 1 to 10. The default is 10.

  • When every voice is in use, a new finger doesn’t play until you lift one.
  • With HOLD on, a new note replaces the oldest held note instead.

Narrow or widen the pad’s range

Section titled “Narrow or widen the pad’s range”

OCTAVES RANGE (Settings → Audio → XY-Pad & Scales) sets how many octaves fit across the pad, from 1 to 8. The default is 4.

  • Fewer octaves gives each note more room, which makes it easier to hit a specific note.
  • More octaves gives a wider range in one sweep, but the notes are narrower targets.

The lowest note moves down as the range grows, so the pad stays centred on the useful middle of the keyboard. The table in Key, scale and octaves lists the range for each setting.

Change the time signature

Section titled “Change the time signature”

The time signature chip in the top bar (4/4) has two parts. Tap the first number to choose beats per bar (1 to 16) and the second to choose the beat unit (2, 4, 8 or 16).

The time signature changes:

  • The metronome, which counts the new number of beats per bar.
  • The arpeggiator, whose speeds are fractions of a bar. At the same BPM, 1/4 is one beat in 4/4 but three quarters of a beat in 3/4.
  • Quantize and Count In, which are measured in bars.
  • The clock counter, which counts bars and beats in the new signature.

LOOP LENGTH is measured in note values, not bars: 1 WHOLE is four quarter notes in any time signature. In 3/4, 4 WHOLE is 16 beats, which is five and a third bars. To fill whole bars in 3/4, use FREE and tap at the bar line, or pick a length that divides evenly.
