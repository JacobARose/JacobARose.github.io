How sound flows through the app

Play: knows synths and loopsDeep: exact behavior

Every sound in Chaos Conductor follows a fixed path. Knowing it explains why a knob affects one thing and not another, and what ends up in a loop.

The stages

Section titled “The stages”

  1. The instruments. The synth and the drums each go through their own level knob, SYNTH and DRUMS, first.

  2. The wet/dry split. The Input Mixer splits each instrument in two. The wet part goes into the Audio Effects chain; the dry part skips it. At 100% wet, the default, everything goes through the effects.

  3. The effects chain. The wet sound goes through the enabled effects, top to bottom, in the order of the cards.

  4. PROG. Everything coming out of the effects passes through PROG, the effects return. Turning PROG down lowers only the effected sound, not the dry parts.

  5. The instrument bus. The effected sound and the dry parts are mixed together here.

  6. MASTER. The instrument bus and the loop playback meet at MASTER, and go to the speakers.

Where the microphone goes

Section titled “Where the microphone goes”

The mic or line input passes through INPUT. Where it goes next depends on FX ONLY (INPUT):

  • On: into the effects chain, so the XY pad shapes your voice or instrument.
  • Off: straight to the instrument bus, dry, mixed with everything else.

What loops record

Section titled “What loops record”

Loop banks record the instrument bus: synth, drums and mic, wet and dry, after their level knobs. Loop playback joins later, at MASTER, so it isn’t recorded again. That’s why overdubbing on a bank never doubles what it already holds.

Two consequences:

  • A loop captures the effects as they sounded when you recorded. Changing an effect afterwards doesn’t change the loop.
  • LOOPS only changes how loud loops play back. To change a loop’s balance of synth and drums, you have to record it again.

What the session recorder records

Section titled “What the session recorder records”

The session recorder records after MASTER: exactly what you hear, loops included.
