Chaos Conductor Guide

A touch instrument that runs in your browser. Play several notes at once on an XY pad locked to a key and scale, while the same gestures move every audio effect you've mapped to its axes. Add a beat with drum pads and a step sequencer, stack parts in loop banks that stay in time, and record the result. No install, no account.

What’s in the app

Section titled “What’s in the app”

XY pad

Left to right sets the note, bottom to top drives the effects. Play up to ten notes at once, one per finger.

Audio effects

11 effects in a chain you can reorder. Map any of their settings to either axis of the pad.

Loops

Four loop banks that stay in time with each other. Overdub, erase and reverse.

Drums

Sixteen pads, two factory kits, your own samples and a 16-step sequencer.

Arpeggiator

Hold notes or pads and the arpeggiator plays them one at a time, in time with the loops.

MIDI out (experimental)

Send the pad’s notes to another app or a hardware synth. This feature has had less testing than the rest of the app.
