What’s in the app

Section titled “What’s in the app”

XY pad

Left to right sets the note, bottom to top drives the effects. Play up to ten notes with ten fingers.

Audio effects

Eleven effects in a chain you can reorder. Map any of their settings to the pad.

Loops

Four loop banks that stay in time with each other. Overdub, erase, reverse.

Drums

Sixteen pads, two factory kits, your own samples and a 16-step sequencer.

Arpeggiator

Hold notes or pads and the arpeggiator plays them one at a time, in time.

MIDI out

Send the pad’s notes to another app or a hardware synth.
